## The data is not ours to licence

Every price in this atlas was collected and published by
**[Jim Vrckovski (@jvrck)](https://github.com/jvrck)** in
**[`jvrck/openrouterlist`](https://github.com/jvrck/openrouterlist)**, also under the MIT Licence.
It originates from [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai)'s public models API, snapshotted every 12
hours since **21 September 2024**.

This project contains no original data. It reads that ledger and draws pictures of it.

**If you reuse the data — the numbers, the CSV export, the baked `atlas.json` — credit
`jvrck/openrouterlist`, not this project.** And please
[give it a star](https://github.com/jvrck/openrouterlist); two years of uninterrupted collection is
the reason any of this can be plotted at all. Nobody can go back and gather 2024 prices now.
